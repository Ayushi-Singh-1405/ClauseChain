# CSCRF Excerpt — VAPT & Incident Reporting Obligations

**Circular:** SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_CSC_EXT/P/CIR/2024/113
**Title:** Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF) for SEBI Regulated Entities (REs)
**Date:** August 20, 2024
**Applies to (this excerpt):** Stock Brokers / Depository Participants

This file contains two independent clause groups. They are not sequential sections of one obligation — treat each as its own source for Segmentation.

## Clause Group A — Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT)
**Source location:** Section 4.3, pp. 48–49

**4.3.** The VAPT scope, periodicity and compliance has been defined in standard DE.CM.S5 and the corresponding guidelines.

**4.3.1.** The VAPT reporting format has been attached at Annexure-A. Along with the VAPT report, SEBI REs shall also submit the declaration from MD/CEO (as given in Annexure-A). Reporting authority for VAPT report submission: Stock Brokers / Depository Participants report to Stock Exchanges / Depositories; IAs report to BASL; MIIs and rest of the REs report to SEBI.

**4.3.2.** REs shall plan their VAPT activity in the beginning of the financial year. No audit cycle shall be left unaudited due to a change in category — any unaudited period shall be included in the current audit cycle. Periodicity: REs identified as "Protected systems" and/or CII by NCIIPC shall complete one VAPT activity (including report submission, closure, and revalidation) in each half of the financial year (April–September and October–March) — i.e., at least twice a year. All other REs shall complete VAPT at least once a year, commencing in the first quarter of the financial year.

**4.3.3.** Timeline for VAPT activity:
- Report submission: within 1 month of completion of VAPT activity, after approval from the respective IT Committee for REs
- Closure of findings identified during VAPT: within 3 months of submission of the VAPT report (a graded approach based on criticality of observations shall be followed)
- Revalidation of VAPT: within 5 months of completion of VAPT

**4.3.4.** Closure of vulnerabilities shall be regularly tracked by the IT Committee for REs. Any open vulnerabilities after 3 months of VAPT activity shall be approved by the IT Committee for REs and closed before the start of the next VAPT exercise. REs are also expected to maintain a risk register, reviewed by the IT Committee for REs.

**4.3.5.** The report of revalidation of VAPT exercise, and open observations, must be placed before the respective IT Committee for REs for confirmation and appropriate directions.

## Clause Group B — Cybersecurity Incident Reporting
**Source location:** Annexure-O, Part B, pp. 199–200

**Annexure-O, Part B, Cl. 1.** Any cyber-attack(s), cybersecurity incident(s), and breach(es) experienced by REs falling under CERT-In Cybersecurity directions shall be notified to SEBI and CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing/detecting such incidents or being brought to notice about such incidents. This information shall be shared with SEBI via email (mkt_incidents@sebi.gov.in) within 6 hours, and via the SEBI Incident Reporting Portal within 24 hours. Stock Brokers/Depository Participants shall also report the incident(s) to their Stock Exchange/Depository, along with SEBI and CERT-In, within 6 hours of noticing/detecting such incidents. Other cybersecurity incidents shall be reported to SEBI, CERT-In, and NCIIPC (as applicable) within 24 hours. Non-reporting when the RE is/was aware of the incident may result in regulatory action.

**Annexure-O, Part B, Cl. 2.** Non-adherence to this SOP attracts regulatory action as per the extant regulations for REs.

**Annexure-O, Part B, Cl. 3.1.** The incident shall be reported on the SEBI Incident Reporting Portal and via email to mkt_incidents@sebi.gov.in by the RE. The incident shall also be reported to CERT-In per their guidelines/regulations/circulars. Entities whose systems are identified as "Critical Information Infrastructure (CII)/protected system" by NCIIPC shall additionally report and inform NCIIPC in a timely manner.

**Annexure-O, Part B, Cl. 3.3.** Timeline for post-cyber-incident activities and reports (measured from the date of reporting the incident or being brought to notice about it):
- Interim Report: 3 days
- Mitigation measures report: 7 days
- Root Cause Analysis (RCA) report: 30 days
- Forensic Audit Report (on the incident) and its closure report: per Cl. 3.4 (not included in this excerpt)
- VAPT for the incident and its closure report: 45 days
- Any other report as required by SEBI: as per SEBI direction
